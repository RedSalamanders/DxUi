- Prepared math and diagrams do less work, and a flowchart edge is now one path (a pixel change on purpose, below). The OpenType MATH data of a font face is shared across geometry generations through the owner thread's cache (fixed storage for eight entries, one per DirectWrite factory and family, each only a weak reference to data the prepared formulas hold, so the thread keeps no factory, collection or font face alive and a thread that prepared nothing holds nothing; replaced when the system font collection changes and dropped by the retained-cache registry as `RichText.mathFontData`), so a formula prepared again reads no font: on `FX8`, whose new phase `formula-insert` types ten characters inside the first formula, the 10 font reads fall to 0 (a prose insert read none before, since the formulas a generation carries lend their data). Which authored colors read on the page colors is decided once per pair of page colors for a colored formula and for a diagram with authored colors, instead of at every paint: the 1,440 relative-luminance evaluations (up to three `std::pow` each) of the 40 prose inserts fall to 0, and a theme change still carries every prepared object. Each flowchart edge's connector is one prepared path stroked with one `DrawGeometry`: the 600 `DrawLine` calls for the 120 edges those inserts draw become 120 draw calls. Pixel change: a dashed edge's pattern now runs on across a bend instead of restarting at each segment, a thick edge is joined at its bends with a bevel join (flat caps; a dotted edge keeps its round dots), whose ink stays inside the ink the segments drew apart at any angle, and an edge of one segment changes a few anti-aliased pixels at its ends; on WARP only pixels a connector paints differ (the four diagram tiles of the gallery's RichTextEditor tiles, 4 to 12 pixels each, and 723 to 2,391 pixels of the flowchart edges tile), while all 150 tiles are identical to the base when connectors are drawn one `DrawLine` per segment, so the font cache, the color decisions and the SVG change move no pixel of any math, flowchart or SVG tile. A preparation rasterizes all its SVG pictures through one private device context (`FX8`, now with two SVG pictures: 2 contexts per step become 1), and the strip size of a large raster is planned at every turn from its pixel count, an estimate of its elements and that turn's whole budget, so a caller that passes a smaller upload budget on a later turn still completes. Painting a colored formula or a diagram allocates nothing, with or without authored colors and on any pair of page colors. New work counters `rich.edgeConnectorDraws`, `rich.edgesDrawn`, `rich.luminanceEvaluations` and `rich.mathFontReads`; the gallery gains the tiles "native C++ colored mathematics", "native C++ flowchart edges" and "SVG picture". Validated by the Embedded suite (`Embedded.Tests.MathDiagramsFx8.h`: the four gate clauses on `FX8`, the font cache following the font collection, the strip plan), the EditorControls and Rendering suites (the incremental-versus-fresh pixel oracle among them), the receipt `Measurements/PerformanceAndMotion_2026-11-13/W7.7` and `validate.ps1`; the `FX8` receipts are re-recorded with the other fixture receipts.
