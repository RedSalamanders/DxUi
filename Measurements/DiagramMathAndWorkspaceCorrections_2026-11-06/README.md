## Result and cause

Class-diagram ranking now follows authored endpoints independently of the inheritance marker. For
`direction LR` and `Document <|-- Markdown`, Document is left of Markdown and the hollow marker is at Document.
The leading marker no longer shortens the opposite end of the connector. Independent raster checks cover
both relationship spellings, marker types and all four directions. Flat acyclic flowcharts with shared fan-in
balance feasible ranks and assign distinct ports, keeping the supplied BT pipeline's Selection and Resources
beside Geometry. Dense many-to-many graphs retain their separate routing policy; this is no claim of complete
Mermaid layout or syntax parity.

Math placement uses measured glyph ink and a positive clearance rather than adding an entire font strut above
or below every decoration. Dots have visible filled area, vector heads scale with their stems, MATH horizontal
variants align to the accent band and the fallback wide tilde is one smooth wave. Matrix row measurement and
placement share an ink-aware strut; gathered/aligned cells use display style, with bounded row gaps and correctly
sized angle delimiters. Geometry regressions cover both math-font and fallback families at three sizes and DPIs.
RedTilde's exact-pin application comparisons are published with its own consumer evidence.

`ExchangeDocumentState` atomically exchanges source, oriented selection, revisions and both history rings
without a text edit. It declines during composition or a transient preview and when editing is disabled or
read-only. Private binding identity retires old geometry/resources and distinguishes equal numeric source
revisions for selection publication. Callback-driven retirement remains guarded. Applications own retained
documents, dirty state, recovery and activation policy.

D3 source editing can cross the old presentation interval. Natural movement or edits retire stale source
presentation; nested-fence Tab uses parsed physical content-line starts without changing container prefixes.
D5 network-file destinations include UNC/scheme-relative paths, every `file://` authority and relative paths
under a UNC base. They require remote-image permission and expose no local `FilePath()`. Resource admission and
transport permission remain separate: the sample loader declines network-file I/O by default, including after
resource opt-in. Native and embedded tests cover current/stale completion and permission revocation.

`VK_PACKET` and bare modifier transitions preserve a pending high surrogate in TextField, RichTextEditor and
editable ComboBox. ComboBox previously published a lead immediately; it now commits the complete scalar once,
with one notification and one undo item. Movement, actual replacement and focus changes cancel pending input.
Hidden native-message and supplied-device replay cover both hosts without taking desktop focus.

Markdown consumes a deferred autolink suffix exactly once. Incremental closed-root reuse checks absolute-start
front-matter context in both directions. YAML privately normalizes CR/CRLF for the parser while retaining exact
public source/ranges; scoped allocation callbacks include the global tag cache and checked Release failures.
The YAML corpus runs with LF, CR and CRLF and distinct-tag allocation failures retry independently.
