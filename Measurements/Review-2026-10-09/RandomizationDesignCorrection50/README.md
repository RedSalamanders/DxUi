# Randomization design correction

The historical generator shuffled exactly six ABBA and six BAAB blocks. Its judge called the analysis an exact
randomization test while using all 4096 independent sign assignments. The physical assignment scheme permits only
C(12,6) = 924 alternatives. The lead independently reviewed the generator, judge, gate, tests and owning contract,
and verified this defect against [the randomization-test requirement](https://arxiv.org/html/1912.02633v2) that the
test reflect the actual assignment scheme. A separate agent reached the same conclusion; no model of independent
symmetric stochastic errors had been declared or established that would justify a different, model-based test.

The repair independently draws either ABBA or BAAB per block, preserving two runs per side, the twelve-block
default, 26-outcome Holm family, investigation bands and exact budgets. Study schema 3, judge v2 and allocation
`independent-ABBA-BAAB-v1` distinguish the repaired design. The gate replays the recorded integer seed and binds
every block, run position and decision to that protocol. Coincidental six/six samples remain eligible. Historical
schemas, missing/old protocol identities and mutated seeds/positions are rejected. The proposed policy stays
unqualified and cannot bootstrap its own trusted approval. Fresh A/A calibration and paired studies remain required.

[The correction script](Reanalyze-AccessibilityAllocation.ps1) separately reanalyses the historical accessibility
study under its actual restricted allocation. It checks all 48 original log hashes, reconstructs the six outcomes'
block effects from retained process medians, checks them against the original recorded effects, and enumerates
every legal order assignment. [The correction](accessibility-correction.json) binds its script and original study
hashes (script text normalizes CRLF to LF, while original study/log/archive hashes cover exact bytes). The lead independently replayed the calculation with recursive six-of-twelve enumeration: all six outcomes
have two extreme alternatives among 924, raw p = 0.0021645021645 and Holm-adjusted p = 0.0129870129870. The original
independent-sign-flip claims (0.00048828125 raw; 0.0029296875 adjusted) are superseded. The observed cost deltas,
snapshot counters, first-query delay and developer's decision to retain coalescing are unchanged. This remains
explanatory evidence and supplies no policy approval or consumer latency allowance.

The first-candidate CI paired packet (`ffc64c8`, run 37993602279, artifact 11647191532) is preserved in
`first-candidate-paired-diagnostic.zip`. The lead checked all 144 receipt hashes and identities, all 78 metric
calculations and the original judge's exact budgets; those checks reproduce the implementation but do not repair
its assignment-design mismatch. All three original scenario sets say within-noise-budget, while the official
verdict is inconclusive/policy-review-required. One legacy/migrated improved-metric disagreement also remains
recorded. None of these labels qualifies the new policy.

The local old-design A/A attempt at `ed70a83` was interrupted after confirmation of the defect. Its completed
48 Default and 13 MultilineGrid receipts, partial reports and parent log are preserved in
`interrupted-aa-diagnostic.zip`; MultilineGridDistinct and the subsequent paired study did not start. There is no
completed A/A result or qualification. The owned driver and benchmark processes were verified stopped, and
independently launched application/build processes were left untouched. `archive-manifest.txt` records every
original archived file and both archive hashes; archive entries were verified byte-for-byte against the originals.
