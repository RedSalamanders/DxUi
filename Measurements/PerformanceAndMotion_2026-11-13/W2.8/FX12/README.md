Generated by `performance-fixtures.ps1` from the receipts in this directory; do not edit by hand. Fixture framework:
[performance fixtures](../../../../Specs/Testing/Testing_Validation.md#performance-fixtures); plan item `W0.4` of the

- **Scenario**: Variants default and uhd-192dpi: 2,000 controls (a 40 x 50 grid of cells cycling three Labels, Button, Checkbox, Toggle, ProgressBar, Slider) filling 1920x1080 DIP, at 1920x1080 and 96 DPI or at 3840x2160 and 192 DPI. typography-120: the same screen with a two-row Tree in place of every eighth cell's Label and every Label and Tree typed by one of 120 distinct (size, weight) typographies. form-10k: 10,000 controls in one ScrollPanel, 2,000 rows of Label, TextField, Checkbox, Toggle and Button. Absolute layout, reduced motion. Phases: full frames, one hover change, one Label text change, wheel scrolling. Per-control memory is the built sample's tracked live bytes over the control count. G5 is a hardware timing verdict: not measured here.
- **Budgets proved**: `G5`, `G15`, `G13`, `G2`
- **Phases**: `warm` (3 steps, warmup): an unchanged frame, then the pointer moves onto the first and the second hover button; `full` (4 steps, measured): ControlHost::Invalidate with nothing changed: a full frame; `hover` (6 steps, measured): the pointer moves between two adjacent buttons: one hover change per frame; `value` (4 steps, measured): one Label's text changes, its bounds unchanged; `scroll` (6 steps, measured): one wheel notch down over the form (form-10k); the dense screen has nothing to scroll, so its frames are unchanged
- **Timing**: filled only by `performance-timing.ps1` on a hardware adapter in a quiet window (W0.2); "not measured" otherwise.
