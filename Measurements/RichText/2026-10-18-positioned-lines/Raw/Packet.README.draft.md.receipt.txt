Draft: final matrix, tooling, sealing and tracked replay are still pending.

The candidate keeps DirectWrite's document-coordinate accumulation inside each
retained chunk with an invisible positioning-only inline object and line break.
Its two private UTF-16 units never enter source/display maps, navigation, text
stores or UIA text. Drawing preserves the platform's caret/font/color behavior;
each layout draws at the same document origin. No custom monochrome renderer,
private Office ABI, embedded worker/timer/HWND/device or production WARP policy
change is introduced.

Reuse additionally requires the same document origin. Height changes may rebuild
the complete dependent suffix and count all that source work. An inexact private
line or empty displayed suffix retains canonical whole geometry and its terminal
font. The chunk graph remains capped at 127 layouts plus an optional whole cache.
The complete Markdown/extension engine, shared/cooperative preparation, universal
incremental work, physical/platform and trusted frame-performance gates remain
open. This component does not finish the full editor.
