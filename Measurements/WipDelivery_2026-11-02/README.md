## Performance and size evidence

The [official paired review](raw/performance/paired-review-01/paired-review.md.txt) compares baseline **4423074dee2410acb28e62b41072e6c561a79eb8** with candidate/harness **21a74896bdc05524ccca106fdfaabe0e96b6477c**. The independent replay checked 144 raw hashes, 936 block effects, 78 metric/Holm rows and 1,872 control observations, with zero mismatches. Both judges reported within-noise for all three scenarios and no regressed rows; 71/72 controls were unstable and 549 observations crossed bands. Policy remains **qualified=false**: this is not a performance acceptance or proof of no regression. The local collector had no continuous whole-host observer. The user-approved quiet window, paused competing work, preflight and three-second CPU observations do not prove whole-interval quiet; a continuous observer is a hosted requirement, and its absence alone does not invalidate this local collection or mandate a rerun. Four project timestamps were restored to their exact original ticks. [All original paired JSON](raw/performance/official-paired/) and [preconditioning records](raw/performance/preconditioning/) are retained.

The separate [64-slot semantic study](raw/performance/semantic/analysis/descriptive-report.json.receipt.txt) is descriptive fixed-orientation ABBA/BAAB evidence. Uninstrumented character edit median is 34,398 to 15,809.5 microseconds; initial owner turns rise 548 to 587, while post-burst turns fall 507 to 259 and edit turns 548 to 317. In-flight edit p95 rises from 35,545 to 50,002 microseconds. Tagged requested C++ new peak is 14,817,702 to 13,506,868 bytes, with 576 bytes after teardown in both; allocations include fixture/caller requests and exclude SDK/GPU/allocator internals, so neither editor-only retention nor leak claims follow. Owner turns are not vsync latency; cancellation of a completed old job alone does not prove a worker restart. [Raw semantic runs](raw/performance/semantic/delivery-01/) and [certificates](raw/performance/semantic/certificates/) are retained; the probe executables, PDBs and original producer artifacts remain private and frozen.

The [same-toolset size record](raw/receipts/main/release-sizes.json.receipt.txt) compares the full feature batch to baseline 442: the LTCG library archive grew from 189,760,522 to 236,095,678 bytes (+24.42%); the EmbeddedControls sample grew from 2,019,840 to 3,586,048 bytes (+77.54%). This is material sample packaging cost, not an installed-library size, a working-set observation or a compiler-only effect.

Source inspection and group dispositions are not a claim of an independent line-by-line audit of every subclaim.
